# Long-Term Retention
## Office of the Chief Product Vision Officer — ImpactOne

**Mandate:** Design how users stay for years — not through mechanics that manufacture continued attention, but because the relationship genuinely deepens as their own life does. `RETENTION_SYSTEM.md` already defines the daily/weekly/monthly/yearly cadence and names the manipulative mechanics this platform refuses to build. This document takes the longer view: what makes someone still be here in year five, and why that's earned rather than engineered.

---

## The Core Idea: The Relationship Changes Shape, Not Just Frequency

A retention system built on manipulation has to work harder every year, because novelty fades and manufactured hooks require ever-larger doses to keep working — the mechanism behind every "engagement" product that burns out its own users over time. A retention system built on genuine value works the opposite way: it requires *less* manufactured effort every year, because the relationship itself deepens. This document describes what that deepening actually looks like, stage by stage.

---

## Year 1: Student

**Why they stay:** The daily habit is forming, trust is being tested and earned one honest moment at a time, and the platform is still mostly teaching vocabulary and pattern recognition (`EDUCATION_STRATEGY.md` Years 1–2).
**What keeps this genuine:** Every trust-building moment in year one is real — an honest "nothing urgent," a graded outcome shown including a miss, an explanation that actually clicked. None of it is manufactured to feel meaningful; it simply is.

## Years 2–3: Practitioner

**Why they stay:** The user now has a real, evolving portfolio or watchlist that reflects their own actual life — this is no longer a generic tool, it's *their* record, with a growing history only this specific relationship has produced. The Monthly Review has become a real ritual, not a novelty.
**What keeps this genuine:** The platform's honesty has now survived at least one real test — a wrong call, a disclosed data gap, a rough patch in the markets — and the user has seen it handled the way `TRUST_RECOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md` describes. This is the stage where trust stops being provisional and starts being based on an actual track record the user has personally witnessed.

## Years 4–5: Independent Judge

**Why they stay:** The user increasingly uses the platform to check their own thinking rather than to be told what to think (`EDUCATION_STRATEGY.md` Year 4) — the relationship has matured from teacher-and-student into something closer to a trusted second opinion between equals.
**What keeps this genuine:** The user checks *less* often, not more, and this is celebrated internally as the product succeeding, never treated as a retention risk to be reversed with more aggressive notifications. A retention strategy that panics when its most successful users need it less has misunderstood its own mission.

## Year 5+: Multi-Generational

**Why they stay:** Life events — a teenager to teach, a parent to help, a retirement to plan for — bring new dimensions of the same relationship into play, through Family/Mentor Mode and the same core product the user has trusted for years. The relationship isn't replaced by a new life stage; it expands to hold it.
**What keeps this genuine:** The platform has never asked this user to be a different kind of customer as their life changed — the same evidence, the same honesty, the same confidence/uncertainty model, simply now serving a parent teaching a teenager the way it once served that same person as a teenager themselves.

---

## What Makes This Different From Manipulated Retention

| Manipulated retention | Genuine long-term retention (this model) |
|---|---|
| Requires escalating hooks over time as novelty fades | Requires less manufactured effort each year as real trust accumulates |
| Treats reduced app-checking as a churn risk to reverse | Treats reduced app-checking by a well-served user as success |
| Depends on the user never fully trusting the product (so they keep checking) | Depends on the user trusting the product more each year (so they check calmly) |
| Breaks the moment a manufactured mechanic is removed | Strengthens even when nothing about the mechanics changes, because the track record keeps growing regardless |

---

## The Only Metric That Matters at This Horizon

Per `PRODUCT_METRICS_SYSTEM.md` and `TRUST_SCORE_MODEL.md`: a cohort's Resilience Trust and Advocacy Trust, tracked across years, not months. A user still active in year five, with rising trust and a demonstrated willingness to bring a family member into the relationship, is the only real proof this document's model is working — and it is a proof that, by design, cannot be faked or accelerated, only earned one honest year at a time.
