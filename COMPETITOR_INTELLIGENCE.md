# ImpactOne Competitor Intelligence

Date: 2026-07-11  
Scope: 20 leading AI investing platforms worldwide (retail and institutional)  
Note: Pricing and packaging can change quickly. Enterprise products often use custom contracts.

## 1. Wealthfront

Strengths
- Mature automation and tax optimization
- Strong user experience for passive investors

Weaknesses
- No embedded human advisor in core product
- Less appealing for active trading users

AI capabilities
- Algorithmic portfolio construction
- Automated rebalancing
- Daily tax-loss harvesting and direct-indexing logic

Pricing
- Around 0.25% AUM for automated investing (with product-specific variations)

Features users love
- Set-and-forget investing
- Tax tools and clean mobile UX

Biggest complaints
- Limited human guidance for complex situations

What ImpactOne should learn from it
- Keep automation strong, but add optional high-touch advisor or expert-assist moments.

## 2. Betterment

Strengths
- Goal-based planning and retirement workflows
- Strong tax coordination features

Weaknesses
- Flat-fee structure can feel expensive for small balances
- Limited advanced customization in lower tiers

AI capabilities
- Goal optimization engines
- Automated rebalancing
- Tax-loss harvesting and tax-coordinated portfolio logic

Pricing
- Digital tier typically around 0.25% AUM or low-balance flat fee; premium tiers higher

Features users love
- Intuitive goals and planning flows
- Reliable autopilot behavior

Biggest complaints
- Fee perception at low balances

What ImpactOne should learn from it
- Make early-stage pricing feel fair for small accounts, not only for high AUM users.

## 3. Fidelity Go

Strengths

- Strong integration with Fidelity accounts

Weaknesses
- Limited thematic and specialty portfolio flexibility

AI capabilities
- Automated allocation and rebalancing
- Portfolio management rules with human oversight

Pricing

Biggest complaints

## Cross-Platform Patterns ImpactOne Should Act On

1. Explainability beats black-box claims.
2. Pricing transparency is part of product quality.
3. Progressive complexity is critical: simple first, advanced later.
4. Workflow depth wins: idea to test to execute to monitor.
5. Most dissatisfaction comes from complexity, hidden costs, and weak onboarding.
