# Mobile Beta Test Plan
## Office of the Mobile Beta Director — ImpactOne

**Mandate:** Define test journeys for five personas against the mobile experience. Each persona is evaluated on the same eight dimensions, so results are comparable across personas rather than five unrelated impressions.

---

## 16-Year-Old Beginner

- **First-open goal:** "Show me something about money I can actually understand."
- **Onboarding expectations:** No jargon before it's explained; a fast path to something real, not a lecture.
- **First 90-second task:** Reach one plain-language, sourced insight and understand it without help (`MORNING_EXPERIENCE_BLUEPRINT.md`).
- **Likely confusion:** Any unexplained term (confidence, uncertainty, exposure) appearing before its inline explanation; a screen with more than one number and no clear hierarchy of what matters most.
- **Trust breakpoint:** A confident-sounding claim that turns out generic or repeated elsewhere — this persona has the least patience for feeling talked down to or lied to.
- **Daily return reason:** A short, honest morning read that makes them feel capable, not overwhelmed.
- **Failure criteria:** Cannot explain, in their own words, what the first screen told them after their first session; abandons before reaching real content.
- **Success criteria:** Completes onboarding to first insight in ≤90 seconds; can correctly explain confidence vs. uncertainty by day 3 of use.

---

## 30-Year-Old Long-Term Investor

- **First-open goal:** "Tell me what's actually relevant to what I own, quickly."
- **Onboarding expectations:** Fast, respectful of their time, willing to skip explanations they don't need.
- **First 90-second task:** Reach a personally-relevant insight tied to their actual holdings/watchlist and judge whether it's genuinely specific to them.
- **Likely confusion:** Generic content presented as personalized; unclear why a recommendation applies to their specific portfolio.
- **Trust breakpoint:** Any claim about "their portfolio" that isn't actually true for their account — the single most damaging failure mode this persona is most likely to notice immediately.
- **Daily return reason:** A short daily habit that reliably tells them whether today requires any action at all.
- **Failure criteria:** Reports the app "feels the same as everything else"; disengages within the first week.
- **Success criteria:** Opens and closes satisfied in under 2 minutes on a majority of days; reports at least one moment the app told them something they didn't already know, specific to their own holdings.

---

## 45-Year-Old Parent

- **First-open goal:** "Is this something I'd trust enough to show my teenager?"
- **Onboarding expectations:** Careful, honest, nothing that feels like it's selling excitement or urgency.
- **First 90-second task:** Judge, within the first session, whether the tone and content are appropriate to hand to a family member.
- **Likely confusion:** Uncertainty about whether the platform is trying to teach responsible habits or drive engagement — this persona reads every screen more skeptically than most, specifically looking for red flags.
- **Trust breakpoint:** Any hint of manufactured urgency, gamification, or hype language — an instant disqualifier for this persona regardless of how good the rest of the experience is.
- **Daily return reason:** A calm, dependable, teachable moment they can eventually share with their teenager.
- **Failure criteria:** Concludes the app is "not something I'd want my kid using" for any reason during the first week.
- **Success criteria:** Explicitly states, unprompted, that they would consider using it with their teenager.

---

## Experienced Investor (self-directed, deliberately skeptical)

- **First-open goal:** "Show me your actual reasoning, not just a verdict."
- **Onboarding expectations:** Wants to skip past basic explanation quickly and get to evidence and methodology.
- **First 90-second task:** Locate the evidence chain, confidence, and uncertainty behind a specific claim without being forced through beginner-oriented content.
- **Likely confusion:** None expected from unclear language — this persona's confusion, if any, comes from missing depth, not missing simplicity.
- **Trust breakpoint:** Any inconsistency between two numbers on the same or adjacent screens, or a confidence score that doesn't visibly vary with the specific evidence presented — this is the persona most likely to notice exactly this failure mode fastest.
- **Daily return reason:** A quick, evidence-rich check that either confirms or challenges their own independent view.
- **Failure criteria:** Identifies templated or non-differentiated content within the first session.
- **Success criteria:** Reports the evidence and reasoning shown changed or reinforced their own thinking at least once during the week.

---

## Retired Investor (new to finance apps generally)

- **First-open goal:** "Help me feel confident, not confused, about my own money."
- **Onboarding expectations:** Patient pacing, large enough text, no assumption of prior app fluency.
- **First 90-second task:** Successfully complete onboarding without needing outside help, and reach one understandable insight.
- **Likely confusion:** Small tap targets, dense information, or any screen requiring rapid or complex gestures.
- **Trust breakpoint:** An unexplained technical failure with no plain-language recovery message — this persona is least likely to assume "it's probably a bug that'll be fixed" and most likely to blame themselves or distrust the app entirely.
- **Daily return reason:** A predictable, calm routine that never rushes them and never assumes prior knowledge.
- **Failure criteria:** Requires outside help to complete onboarding; reports feeling "lost" at any point in the first session.
- **Success criteria:** Completes onboarding independently; can locate and use the Help Center or an inline explanation without assistance by day 3.
