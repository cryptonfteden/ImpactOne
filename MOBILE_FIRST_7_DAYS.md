# Mobile First 7 Days
## Office of the Mobile Beta Director — ImpactOne

**Mandate:** Design the private mobile beta experience for Days 0 through 7. No manipulative notifications, no streaks, no false urgency, anywhere in this sequence — every touchpoint below is checked against that rule before it's included.

---

## Day 0 — Invitation

A personal, named invitation (never a mass message), explaining specifically why this person was chosen and what installing the mobile app will involve. No countdown, no "limited spots" framing — the invitation is honest about being early and rough, per `PRIVATE_BETA_PLAYBOOK.md` §4.

## Day 0 — Installation

A direct install link, a brief note on what device/OS is supported, and a clear statement that this is a private beta build, not the public app. No forced account creation before install completes.

## Day 0 — Onboarding

The standard onboarding journey (`MOBILE_PRODUCT_MASTERPLAN.md` §1), reaching one real, sourced insight within 90 seconds, with a persistent, dismissible beta banner linking to feedback and trust-reporting from the very first screen.

## Day 1 — First Morning

The first real daily-brief experience, at a time the participant chose during onboarding. Content is genuinely current, never padded to appear more eventful than the day actually was. No notification is sent if nothing genuinely material occurred.

## Day 2 — Second Morning

The habit's second occurrence — deliberately not different from Day 1 in tone or structure, so the participant starts to feel the *rhythm* of the product rather than a series of novel, attention-grabbing moments. If Day 1 was quiet and Day 2 is also quiet, both are shown as genuinely quiet, honestly.

## Day 3–4 — First Missing-Data Event

The first time a data source is delayed, incomplete, or unavailable during the beta window (naturally occurring, never artificially staged to "test" the participant without their knowledge). The app shows the honest fallback state (`TRUST_RECOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md`'s "when providers fail" playbook) — what's missing, since when, and what's still reliable. This is treated as a genuine trust-building moment, not an incident to hide.

## Day 5 — First Recommendation Review

The participant is guided, once, to the Recommendations detail view and encouraged to explore the full evidence chain, confidence/uncertainty pair, and invalidation condition — not forced, simply pointed toward a part of the app they may not have found organically yet.

## Day 6 — First Feedback Request

The first structured weekly reflection (`PRIVATE_BETA_PLAYBOOK.md` §7), framed as a genuine two-way conversation, not a mandatory survey — five minutes, three open questions, and the nine tracked scores.

## Day 7 — Review

A real, individual check-in from the team, referencing what the participant actually reported, not a generic "thanks for your first week" message. The team's own internal Day-7 review reads the full week against `MOBILE_ACCEPTANCE_SCORECARD.md` and `MOBILE_TRUST_AUDIT.md`, not just the participant's stated impressions.

---

## What This Sequence Never Includes

- No streak counter, no "don't break your streak" messaging.
- No countdown timers, no "X spots remaining," no artificial scarcity anywhere in the invitation or onboarding flow.
- No notification whose primary purpose is re-engagement rather than genuinely new, relevant content.
- No gamified reward for simply opening the app on consecutive days.

Every day in this sequence is designed to be honestly what it is — a quiet day is shown as quiet, a missing-data event is disclosed as it happens, and the participant's actual first week is what gets reviewed, never a manufactured version of it.
