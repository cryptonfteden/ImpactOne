# CEO — Next 12 Months
## Product and Execution Priorities, Quarter by Quarter

**Mandate:** One year, four quarters, product and execution only — no architecture, no code. Every quarter has one job. A quarter does not start its job until the previous quarter's is actually done, confirmed the same measurable way `BETA_READINESS_AUDIT.md` and `PRIVATE_BETA_GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` already confirm things — never on a calendar assumption.

---

## Quarter 1 — Earn the Right to Have Users

**The only job this quarter:** close every Critical finding already on record, then run the 25-person private beta to completion.

- Fix the product's reachability defect until it passes `PRIVATE_BETA_GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` Section A in full — no exceptions, no partial credit.
- Eliminate the false personalized-claim failure mode and the templated Daily Feed/Alerts content, extending the quality bar already proven on the Recommendations screen to everywhere else it's missing.
- Ship real onboarding.
- Extend the confidence/uncertainty dual score and explicit invalidation conditions everywhere a directional claim appears.
- Independently re-verify AI Analysis, Themes, Notifications, Profile, and Settings — currently unconfirmed.
- Recruit, onboard, and run the full 25-person private beta (`PRIVATE_BETA_PLAYBOOK.md`) for its full 4-week cycle, with weekly CEO Dashboard reviews throughout.

**Quarter 1 is done when:** `PRIVATE_BETA_GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` passes at 100% across all four sections, and the beta itself hits its own stated success metrics (≥15 of 25 retained 4+ days/week by week 4, ≥18 of 25 at trust rating 6+/10). Nothing else this document lists may begin before this is true.

---

## Quarter 2 — Prove It Travels Without a Founder in the Room

**The only job this quarter:** grow from 25 to 250 real users (`FIRST_1000_USERS_PLAN.md` Stages 2–3), and prove the product's trust and clarity hold up with zero personal vouching.

- Scale to 100 users first, closing that stage's gate (day-30 retention ≥70%, flat-or-rising cohort trust) before advancing further.
- Advance to 250 users only once genuine strangers — no personal connection to the team — begin arriving through content and university-club channels.
- Ship the visible, honest track-record/calibration screen — the single highest-leverage trust feature the product doesn't yet have anywhere a user can see it.
- Ship the Monthly Portfolio Review surface.
- Fix real portfolio exposure computation (sector/concentration), replacing placeholder-style output.
- Consolidate navigation from twelve destinations toward the simpler model already specified, closing the Dashboard/Global Intelligence/Watchlist/Alerts redundancies identified in `FEATURE_PRIORITY_MATRIX.md`.

**Quarter 2 is done when:** 250 real users are active, day-30 retention for stranger-acquired users is within 10 points of the personal-network baseline, and the track-record screen has been viewed by a majority of active users at least once.

---

## Quarter 3 — Build the Habit at Real Scale

**The only job this quarter:** grow from 250 to 1,000 users (`FIRST_1000_USERS_PLAN.md` Stages 4–5), and get the full daily/weekly/monthly/yearly retention system (`RETENTION_SYSTEM.md`) running end to end for the first time.

- Ship Family/Mentor Mode, tested first with a deliberately recruited subset before general availability.
- Ship the trust-triggered, non-cash referral mechanism.
- Ship alternate tone/complexity modes ("explain like I'm new," "explain like I'm 65") to widen the addressable audience without fragmenting the underlying truth model.
- Complete the accessibility pass (dynamic type, screen-reader labels, contrast) — overdue by this point and non-negotiable before wider growth.
- Run the first full monthly-review cycle across the entire active user base, not just a sample.

**Quarter 3 is done when:** 1,000 real users are active, Weekly Trust-Confirmed Users is trending upward as a share of total users, and the platform has its first genuinely meaningful, cohort-wide calibration data — real evidence, not an estimate, of whether stated confidence corresponds to real-world accuracy.

---

## Quarter 4 — Prepare for the World, Not Just the Next Thousand

**The only job this quarter:** decide, with evidence, whether the product is ready to leave closed, invitation-based growth — and if so, prepare the first genuinely public-facing moment without compromising anything closed growth protected.

- Publish the platform's first annual "what we got right and wrong this year" report — the most important single artifact this quarter produces, and the clearest test yet of whether the honesty commitment holds at real scale, in public, for the first time.
- Run a full, honest go/no-go review of public-beta readiness, structured the same way `BETA_READINESS_AUDIT.md` structured private-beta readiness — evidence only, no opinions, Critical findings block advancement regardless of growth pressure.
- Begin the first careful, narrow test of premium/optional monetization — never gating the free evidence/confidence/uncertainty core, exactly as `GROWTH_PLAYBOOK.md` commits to permanently.
- Formalize the university and content partnership channels that have been informally working since Quarter 2 into a real, resourced program.
- Complete a full-year retrospective against every principle named in `VISION.md` and every metric named in `RETENTION_SYSTEM.md` and `GROWTH_PLAYBOOK.md` — an honest accounting of what held, what didn't, and what this document's own quarterly plan got wrong, in the same spirit the product asks of itself every month.

**Quarter 4 is done when:** the annual report has shipped, the public-beta go/no-go decision has been made on evidence alone, and the year closes with a written, honest record of what actually happened against what this document predicted — filed the same way this platform files every other graded outcome, mistakes included.

---

## The One Rule That Governs All Four Quarters

No quarter's user-growth target is ever hit by relaxing a trust, honesty, or advisory-only commitment established in any prior document this year. If a quarter is behind its growth number and on track on trust, that quarter is a success. If a quarter is ahead of its growth number and behind on trust, that quarter is a failure — regardless of how the rest of the year looks on a chart.
