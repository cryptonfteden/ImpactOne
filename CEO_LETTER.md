# A Letter to the CEO

Dear CEO,

I spent real time with your app this week, the way I'd spend time with any app I was deciding whether to actually keep. I want to tell you the truth, not the polished version.

The best parts of your product are genuinely good. When it's working, opening it feels calm — it tells me what happened, whether it matters to me specifically, and whether I need to do anything today. Most finance apps are trying to make me anxious enough to keep checking them. Yours, on its best days, does the opposite, and that's rare enough that I noticed it immediately and liked it.

But I need to tell you about the day I'm writing this letter on: I opened your app, and nothing happened. No brief, no error, no explanation — just an empty screen with a search bar. If this had been my first time ever opening it, I would have deleted it on the spot, and I would never have known that the calm, honest version I described above even existed. You cannot build a habit on top of an app that sometimes just doesn't show up.

I also found something that worried me more than the blank screen. I opened a recommendation and saw a panel of five "experts." Every one of them said Reduce or Hold. Right above their names, in large letters, the app told me to Buy anyway. Nobody explained why. I don't know if that's a bug or a design choice, but I know exactly what it did to me as a reader: the instant I noticed it, I stopped trusting the headline, and I started reading everything else on the page more suspiciously too. That's the opposite of what a panel of experts is supposed to do for me.

Here's my honest advice, not as someone who builds software, but as someone deciding whether to trust you with how I think about my own money: fix reliability first, so the app is never blank. Fix the panel so it either agrees with itself or explains why it doesn't. And then get out of your own way — cut the screens and buttons that don't obviously earn their place, because every one of them is a small tax on my trust before I've even gotten to the good part.

You have something real here. I noticed it. I just can't rely on it yet.

Sincerely,
A private investor who wants to delete the other four apps on my phone, and isn't quite ready to yet.
