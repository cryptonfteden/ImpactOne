## Overall Read

Individually, most of these scores would place this product ahead of typical seed-stage fintech AI competitors on substance. The one score that overrides the rest is reliability for a new user — a product this promising cannot be judged "world-class" while it cannot reliably show itself to someone seeing it for the first time. This is not a ceiling on the product's potential; it is a ceiling on what can honestly be claimed about it **today**.

## What Would Raise Every Score Above

Fixing the new-user crash doesn't just fix one bug — it unlocks the ability to actually verify Decision Center, Executive-Dashboard-style consolidation, and the chart ecosystem for the exact audience (new users, new investors) this review is written for. Today's scores for those items are informed estimates from the last working session, not fresh confirmations — the single highest-leverage next step is closing that gap so every score above can be re-earned with direct evidence rather than carried forward.
