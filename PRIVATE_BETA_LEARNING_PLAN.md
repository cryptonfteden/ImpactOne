# Private Beta Learning Plan
## Office of the Global Product Board — ImpactOne

**Mandate:** Define exactly what the team should learn from the first 5 users — limited strictly to the highest-value questions. Five people cannot produce statistically meaningful trends; what they can produce, if watched closely, is depth no larger cohort will ever offer again. This plan is deliberately short — a long list of questions at this scale dilutes attention across too many half-answered signals, exactly the failure `BETA_FEEDBACK_ANALYSIS.md` warns against.

---

## The Six Questions, and Only These Six

1. **Does a real user notice the platform's known open trust issues without being told to look for them?** Specifically: does a genuine account holder, using the app naturally, ever encounter the "portfolio overlap" claim or a repeated explanation pattern — and if so, what is their unprompted, in-the-moment reaction? This is the single highest-value question available at this scale, because it can only ever be answered by direct observation of a real person, once, before the issue is fixed.

2. **Does the new mobile layout and onboarding actually get someone from install to a genuine "I understand what this is" moment, unassisted?** Watched directly, not self-reported.

3. **Does the Daily Feed's new evidence — historical analogies, counter-evidence, invalidation conditions — get read, or ignored?** Observed via what a participant actually taps and lingers on, not what they say they'd read.

4. **Does the participant return, unprompted, the next morning?** The single cleanest behavioral signal this framework has for whether daily value (`DAILY_VALUE_FRAMEWORK.md`) is genuinely present, independent of anything they say in an interview.

5. **Where does stated trust diverge from observed behavior for the same person?** The say-vs-do gap (`TRUST_SCORE_MODEL.md`) is more informative with 5 closely-watched people than with 500 loosely-surveyed ones — this is exactly the kind of signal small scale is uniquely suited to catch.

6. **What causes hesitation — a pause, a re-read, a reach for another app — that the participant never mentions unprompted?** Captured only through direct observation (`MOBILE_BETA_INTERVIEW_SCRIPT.md`), because participants routinely fail to self-report their own hesitation even when asked directly.

---

## What This Plan Deliberately Does Not Try to Learn Yet

- Quantitative retention curves, conversion rates, or any statistic requiring a real sample size — five people cannot support this, and treating early numbers as trend data would be a methodology error this platform's own principles explicitly forbid.
- Broad feature-request gathering — five people's preferences are not a roadmap; premature roadmap-shaping from this cohort risks exactly the single-vocal-participant distortion `BETA_FEEDBACK_ANALYSIS.md` warns against.
- Localization or international feedback — entirely out of scope at this stage and scale.
- Long-term retention or multi-month habit formation — five days to a few weeks cannot answer a multi-year question.

---

## How These Six Questions Are Answered

Exclusively through the direct, in-person or closely-observed method in `MOBILE_BETA_INTERVIEW_SCRIPT.md` — silent observation first, structured interview second — never through a survey link sent asynchronously. At this scale, the entire value of the exercise depends on the team actually watching, not just collecting self-reported scores. A learning plan this narrow, executed with real depth, will teach the team more than a broad plan executed at a survey's arm's length ever could.
