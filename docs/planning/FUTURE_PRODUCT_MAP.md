# Future Product Map
## Office of the Chief Product Vision Officer — ImpactOne, at One Million Users

**Mandate:** Assume ImpactOne reaches one million users. What should the product look like then? Which principles must never change, and which parts of the product are expected — even required — to evolve? A company that cannot answer this clearly risks either freezing today's product in place out of fear, or drifting away from itself one convenient exception at a time.

---

## What Must Never Change

- **Advisory-only, structurally enforced.** No engine, no feature, no partnership ever gains the ability to place a real trade on a user's behalf.
- **One canonical verdict.** However many subsystems, personas, or engines exist at this scale, exactly one reconciled answer ever reaches a user for a given decision — never two disagreeing ones standing side by side.
- **Confidence and uncertainty as two independent, honestly-labeled numbers.** Never collapsed into one dial, at any scale, for any audience, regardless of how much simpler a blended score would be to explain to a mainstream audience.
- **An honest, gradable track record, including every miss.** At one million users, this record is the platform's single largest asset — and the temptation to obscure a bad stretch will be proportionally larger, not smaller, which is exactly why this principle must be the least negotiable of all of them.
- **The free evidence-and-honesty core.** Whatever monetization exists at this scale, confidence, uncertainty, evidence, and the track record remain free, permanently, for everyone.
- **No dark patterns, no manufactured urgency, no engagement-optimized notification design** — regardless of how much a growth team at this scale could plausibly demonstrate these would "work."
- **Personalization changes tone and relevance, never the underlying truth** (`PERSONALIZATION_PRINCIPLES.md`) — a teenager and a retired professional portfolio manager see the same evidence and the same verdict for the same claim, always.

---

## What Should Evolve

- **Screens and navigation.** The current twelve-destination sidebar has no business existing at one million users; the interface should be simpler, not more complex, than it is today — depth increases, surface-level navigational complexity should not.
- **Education, from contextual to deeply adaptive.** At this scale, the platform likely serves genuinely distinct sub-populations (teens, professionals, retirees, non-English speakers) whose educational needs diverge further than they do today — `INVESTOR_PSYCHOLOGY_MODEL.md`'s four stages become the foundation of a much more finely adaptive system, still contextual, never a course.
- **Community, from a small private channel to a real, self-moderating structure.** Long-tenured users mentoring new ones at scale, per `RETENTION_SYSTEM.md` and `GROWTH_PLAYBOOK.md`'s Stage 6 vision — moderated by trained, trusted long-time users, not a hired team unfamiliar with the platform's tone.
- **Coverage breadth.** More asset classes, more international markets — each genuinely localized, with real local evidence sources, never just translated UI over the same US-only data.
- **The Knowledge Graph and World Memory, at genuine scale.** Billions of events, a truly temporal, decade-spanning record — the design in `KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_ARCHITECTURE.md` was written specifically to still be correct at this scale, and this is the point where that design gets to prove it.
- **Personalization depth.** Far more nuanced adaptation to a user's specific life stage and psychology (`INVESTOR_PSYCHOLOGY_MODEL.md`), while the boundary of what personalization is allowed to touch stays exactly where `PERSONALIZATION_PRINCIPLES.md` already draws it.
- **Monetization sophistication.** Multiple tiers, family/household plans, and aligned institutional or education partnerships — evolving in structure, never in the commitment that the honest core stays free.
- **The research and reasoning organization itself.** Whatever the underlying reasoning architecture has become by this scale — more analysts, more departments, more specialized subsystems — it must still, per `CANONICAL_DOMAIN_MODEL.md`'s standing rule, resolve into exactly one canonical verdict, never a second independently-computed one.

---

## The Test for Every Future Product Decision at This Scale

Before any change ships once the platform reaches one million users, it must answer: **does this increase reach, depth, or relevance — or does it touch the underlying truth, the honesty of the track record, or the advisory-only boundary?** The first category is where this platform is expected, even required, to keep evolving aggressively. The second category is exactly the same six-item list this document opens with, unchanged from a million users to a hundred, to a billion — because those are not features of this product. They are what makes it this product at all.
