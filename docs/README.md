## Archive

The archive contains point-in-time evidence. Archived documents describe what was assessed or delivered at a particular moment; they are not automatically current requirements.

- `archive/sprints/` — sprint, phase, and milestone records
- `archive/releases/` — beta and release-candidate evidence
- `archive/audits/` — audits, reviews, reports, verdicts, certifications, scorecards, and sign-offs

## Status definitions

- **Canonical:** maintained source of truth.
- **Supporting:** durable context, but not authoritative.
- **Archive:** historical point-in-time evidence.
- **Duplicate:** identical exported material pending deduplication.
- **Undecided:** requires canonical-versus-superseded review.

The first reorganization pass intentionally preserves all content. Deduplication and consolidation happen only after canonical documents are selected.
