# USER_INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md

**Phase G1 — Beta Success Review**
**Audience:** the founder, talking directly to each of the five beta users. Every conversation below is meant to be live (call, video, or in person) — never replaced by a written form. Keep each one short enough that it never feels like an obligation: roughly 10 minutes on Day 1, 15 on Day 7, 20 on Day 14.

**Ground rules for all three conversations:**
- Ask open questions, then stay quiet. The founder's job is to listen, not to explain or defend the product.
- Never ask "did you like it?" — it invites politeness. Ask what they *did*, what they *expected*, and what surprised them.
- Write down their exact words for anything surprising, not a paraphrase or a score. A specific sentence is worth more than a 1-10 rating.
- If a user cancels or goes quiet, that itself is data — follow up personally within 24 hours, don't just reschedule silently.

---

## Day 1 — The First Impression Conversation

**Purpose:** capture what they expected before reality had a chance to shape their memory, and check whether the product's opening moments matched it.

1. "Before you opened the app for the first time, what did you think it was going to be?"
2. "Walk me through what actually happened, in your own words, from the moment you opened it."
3. "Was there a moment in that first few minutes where you thought 'wait, what does this mean' or 'is this broken'?"
4. "Did anything make you trust it a little, right away? Did anything make you trust it a little less?"
5. "If a friend asked you right now, on day one, what this app is for — what would you tell them?"
6. "Is there anything you expected to see that you didn't?"

**What the founder is really listening for:** whether the gap between expectation and reality was small (good) or large (a positioning/marketing problem, not a product problem), and whether the very first moments created confusion or confidence.

---

## Day 7 — The Habit Conversation

**Purpose:** find out whether the product earned a real place in the person's actual week, or was quietly ignored — and catch anyone who's already disengaging before it becomes a lost cause.

1. "How many days this week did you open it without me reminding you?"
2. "Tell me about the last time you opened it — what were you doing right before, and what made you open it right then?"
3. "Was there a day you thought about opening it and didn't? What stopped you?"
4. "Has anything it told you turned out to be right, wrong, or somewhere in between? How did that feel?"
5. "Has it changed anything about how you think about your money or the news, even slightly?"
6. "If it disappeared tomorrow, would you actually miss it, or would your week look the same?"
7. "What's the one thing that's annoyed you the most so far — even something small?"

**What the founder is really listening for:** real, unprompted usage triggers (question 2 is the single most valuable question of the whole guide — it's the closest a founder can get to observing the actual moment of need in someone's real life). Question 6 is the honest retention check; a hesitant or lukewarm answer here is more informative than any dashboard number.

---

## Day 14 — The Verdict Conversation

**Purpose:** the conversation that actually tells the founder whether there's a business here. Ask every one of these to every user, even if some feel repetitive with earlier check-ins — the answers often change by the end.

1. "Thinking back over the two weeks — what's the one moment that stands out most, good or bad?"
2. "Would you pay for this? If yes, what would feel fair to pay? If no, what would have to be different?"
3. "Who specifically in your life would you tell to try this? And who would you tell not to bother with it yet?"
4. "What did you think this app would never do, that it turned out to do — or the reverse?"
5. "Did you ever fully understand that it never places a trade or acts on your behalf by itself? Walk me through what you understood that to mean."
6. "If you could change exactly one thing about it, what would it be?"
7. "Compared to whatever you were doing before — a friend, a different app, just the news, nothing at all — is this actually better for you, or just different?"
8. "Is there anything you were afraid to tell me during the last two weeks because it felt too minor or too harsh to mention?"

**What the founder is really listening for:** question 2 is the single most important question in the entire beta — everything else is a proxy for whether this is a real business, and this one asks it directly. Question 8 exists because people routinely hold back their sharpest, most useful criticism unless explicitly invited to give it at the very end, when the relationship feels established enough to be honest.

---

## After Each Round

Immediately after each conversation — same day, before memory fades — the founder should write down, in one or two sentences per person, in their own words:
- The single most surprising thing this person said.
- Whether this person's stated feeling and their actual behavior (opens, follow-through) matched or diverged.
- One direct quote worth remembering.

This short note, repeated five times after each of the three rounds, is worth more than any dashboard the beta could produce.
