# Growth Playbook
## Office of the Chief Growth Officer — ImpactOne

**Mandate:** Design the growth strategy from 1 user to 1,000,000 users. Growth here is defined the way `VISION.md` defines the product itself — never optimized for engagement or frequency for its own sake, always for trust that compounds. A growth playbook that grows the user count while eroding the trust moat is, by this document's own standard, a failed playbook regardless of the numbers.

**North Star Metric (all stages):** ***Weekly Trust-Confirmed Users*** — users who, in the past 7 days, both opened the app and encountered at least one honestly-delivered, verifiable claim (a sourced insight, a graded outcome, an explicit "we don't know"). This is deliberately not "weekly active users" — a user who opens the app and sees nothing verifiable hasn't had a trust-confirming session, and this metric refuses to count it as a win.

---

## Stage 1: 1 User

- **Acquisition:** Founder-built, for a single real, named beginner (see `FIRST_100_USERS_PLAYBOOK.md`, User #1). No channel — a direct relationship.
- **Activation:** Success = this one person opens the app unprompted on day 2 without being asked to.
- **Retention:** Daily personal check-in, informally, by the team.
- **Referral:** None expected or sought yet.
- **Revenue:** None. Revenue at this stage would distort every decision that should be made for trust reasons alone.
- **Education:** Entirely contextual, refined in real time based on this one user's actual confusion points.
- **Community:** None — a single relationship, not a community.
- **Content strategy:** None yet.
- **University strategy:** None yet.
- **Family strategy:** If User #1 is a parent/teen pair, this *is* the family strategy's first real test case.
- **International:** Not applicable.
- **KPIs:** Qualitative only — does this person come back, and can they articulate what the product is for, in their own words, without being told.
- **Leading indicators:** Voluntary day-2 return. Unprompted, accurate description of what the app does.
- **Lagging indicators:** None meaningful at n=1.

## Stage 2: 100 Users

Per `FIRST_100_USERS_PLAYBOOK.md` in full. Summary for this stage's growth-specific dimensions:

- **Acquisition:** Personal network only, deliberately mixed for demographic stress-testing (age, experience level, skepticism).
- **Activation:** First sourced insight within 90 seconds of first open (`MOBILE_PRODUCT_MASTERPLAN.md` §1).
- **Retention:** Personal, high-touch check-ins at day 7 and day 30.
- **Referral:** Organic only, never solicited with an incentive yet — an incentivized referral at this stage would contaminate the read on genuine product-market resonance.
- **Revenue:** Still none. This stage exists to prove trust, not monetization.
- **Education:** Fully contextual, no curriculum.
- **Community:** A single small private channel, team-accessible.
- **Content strategy:** None public yet — internal learnings only.
- **University strategy:** None yet.
- **Family strategy:** Explicitly recruit 2–3 parent/teen or mentor/mentee pairs into this cohort deliberately, to validate the family use case with real people before building any dedicated feature for it.
- **International:** Not applicable.
- **KPIs:** Day-30 retention ≥ 90% for personally-recruited users; day-30 retention for organic strangers (User #100-equivalent) is the first real signal, tracked separately.
- **Leading indicators:** % of users completing onboarding to first real insight; % experiencing a graded outcome within 30 days.
- **Lagging indicators:** Day-30 and day-60 retention; unprompted referral actions.

## Stage 3: 1,000 Users

- **Acquisition:** First deliberate but narrow channel: a small number of genuinely aligned content creators/educators (personal finance educators focused on beginners, not finance influencers focused on hot picks) and 1–2 university personal-finance or investing clubs, chosen for cultural fit over reach.
- **Activation:** Same 90-second-to-first-insight bar, now measured systematically rather than observed anecdotally.
- **Retention:** Personal check-ins no longer scale; replaced by the Monthly Portfolio Review surface (`MOBILE_PRODUCT_MASTERPLAN.md` §8) as the primary trust-reinforcement mechanism, supplemented by a small, real support team reading every piece of feedback personally.
- **Referral:** A first, gentle, trust-triggered referral prompt is introduced (tied to a graded-correct outcome or an explicit "helpful" action — never generic), still uncompensated.
- **Revenue:** Still none, or a first honest test of willingness-to-pay via a simple, optional, clearly-labeled premium tier concept — tested, not shipped, at this stage.
- **Education:** First lightweight, opt-in "learning path" for genuinely new users, built from the most common contextual questions observed in Stages 1–2.
- **Community:** A small, public-but-unadvertised community space (not in-app) opens, moderated directly by the team.
- **Content strategy:** First public content: plain-language explainers of real, current market events, published under the same evidence/confidence/uncertainty discipline as the product itself — content *is* the product's values, demonstrated publicly.
- **University strategy:** Formal outreach begins to 3–5 university investing/finance clubs — not as a marketing channel but because students are a genuinely underserved beginner audience with natural word-of-mouth density (dorms, clubs, group chats).
- **Family strategy:** First dedicated, lightweight "mentor view" concept tested with the parent/teen pairs recruited in Stage 2.
- **International:** Not applicable — English-language, single-market focus remains deliberate.
- **KPIs:** Weekly Trust-Confirmed Users as a share of total registered users; day-30 retention holding steady as the user base diversifies past personal network.
- **Leading indicators:** % of new users from non-personal-network channels who reach first graded outcome within 30 days.
- **Lagging indicators:** Retention curves by acquisition source, compared against the Stage 1–2 personal-network baseline.

## Stage 4: 10,000 Users

- **Acquisition:** Content and university strategy scale deliberately: a regular cadence of plain-language market explainers, a formalized (still unpaid, ambassador-style) university club partnership program, and the first, carefully-scoped paid channel test (never broad-reach finance-influencer sponsorship — narrow, values-aligned partners only).
- **Activation:** Onboarding is now instrumented rigorously; the 90-second-to-insight and 7-tap-maximum bars from `MOBILE_PRODUCT_MASTERPLAN.md` are hard product gates, not aspirations.
- **Retention:** The Monthly Portfolio Review and honest track-record surface become the primary retention engine at scale; personal check-ins shift to a targeted, at-risk-user model rather than universal outreach.
- **Referral:** A structured but modest referral mechanism launches (e.g., both parties unlock an early look at a new feature — never a cash incentive, which would attract low-trust, high-churn users at exactly the wrong moment).
- **Revenue:** First real, optional premium tier ships — deeper portfolio analytics, expanded theme coverage — never gating the core honesty features (evidence, confidence, uncertainty, track record) behind payment, ever, at any stage.
- **Education:** A structured, multi-level learning path (beginner/intermediate) is fully built out, still entirely optional and contextual.
- **Community:** The public community space is now moderated with the first set of community guidelines, explicitly banning performance-bragging and hype language — enforcing the platform's own tone in its community, not just its product.
- **Content strategy:** A weekly published "here's what we got right and wrong" piece — the platform's honesty principle extended into public content, becoming a genuine differentiator versus every competitor who only publicizes wins.
- **University strategy:** Expanded to 15–20 campuses; a lightweight "campus ambassador" role (unpaid or small stipend, values-vetted) for the most engaged student users.
- **Family strategy:** Formal "Family/Mentor Mode" ships as a real, permissioned feature (per `MOBILE_PRODUCT_MASTERPLAN.md` Tier 3 improvement #27).
- **International:** First scoping work begins (research only) into which non-US, English-speaking markets have the most underserved beginner-investor populations — no launch yet.
- **KPIs:** Weekly Trust-Confirmed Users; premium conversion rate among users with ≥2 graded outcomes viewed (a proxy for "trust converts to willingness-to-pay," not "engagement converts to willingness-to-pay").
- **Leading indicators:** Referral participation rate among users who've seen a graded-correct outcome.
- **Lagging indicators:** 90-day and 180-day retention; premium tier churn (a leading warning sign if premium users churn faster than free users — a signal something was oversold).

## Stage 5: 100,000 Users

- **Acquisition:** A genuine, multi-channel content and partnership engine, still governed by the same values-fit filter as Stage 3–4 — reach is never allowed to override alignment. Paid acquisition is now a real, measured channel, but capped by a strict payback-period and quality bar (a paid user must retain and trust-confirm at parity with organic users, or the channel is cut regardless of its cost-per-install).
- **Activation:** Fully instrumented, continuously A/B-refined onboarding — but every experiment is evaluated against Weekly Trust-Confirmed Users, never raw signup conversion alone, so a "better-converting" onboarding that produces less-trusting users is rejected.
- **Retention:** A dedicated Trust & Calibration team (product + data, not growth) owns the track-record and calibration surfaces as a first-class, resourced product area, not an afterthought bolted onto a growth team's roadmap.
- **Referral:** Referral becomes a meaningfully sized acquisition channel, still uncompensated in cash terms, expanded to family/group referral flows (a parent inviting multiple children, a study group).
- **Revenue:** Multiple tiers, and the first serious look at a genuinely aligned B2B2C channel (e.g., partnering with schools or financial-literacy nonprofits) rather than only direct-to-consumer.
- **Education:** A full, structured curriculum, potentially licensable to schools — education becomes a strategic asset, not just an onboarding aid.
- **Community:** Regional/interest-based sub-communities (by theme, by experience level) emerge organically; moderation scales via trained community moderators drawn from the most trusted long-tenured users, not hired strangers unfamiliar with the platform's tone.
- **Content strategy:** A full editorial function exists, still bound by the same evidence/confidence/uncertainty discipline as the product — content is treated as a governed, gradable product surface, not a marketing department operating by different rules.
- **University strategy:** Formal partnerships with university financial-literacy programs; ImpactOne curriculum content integrated into actual coursework at a handful of pilot institutions.
- **Family strategy:** Family/Mentor Mode is a mature, widely-used feature; household-level (not just parent/teen) use cases are supported.
- **International:** First real international launch (one or two carefully chosen markets), localized fully — language, but also source-tier and evidence-provider coverage genuinely adapted, never just translated UI over US-only data.
- **KPIs:** Weekly Trust-Confirmed Users; calibration accuracy trend (publicly reported); premium and B2B2C revenue as a share of total.
- **Leading indicators:** Curriculum completion rates; community moderator-to-user ratio holding steady (a proxy for community health scaling sustainably).
- **Lagging indicators:** Annual cohort retention curves; public calibration-accuracy trend line (this platform's version of a durable brand asset).

## Stage 6: 1,000,000 Users

- **Acquisition:** A recognized, trusted brand in its category, where organic/referral acquisition meaningfully exceeds paid — the clearest sign the trust moat (`COMPANY_STRATEGY_REVIEW.md`'s central thesis) has actually compounded rather than merely been claimed.
- **Activation:** Onboarding is localized and personalized at a level that reflects genuinely different needs (a 16-year-old, a 70-year-old, a first-generation investor, a small-business owner) without ever fragmenting the single, canonical evidence/confidence/uncertainty model underneath — personalization changes tone and emphasis, never the underlying truth claim (per `TRUTH.md` §13.10, still binding at this scale).
- **Retention:** A mature, multi-year track record is now the primary retention asset; the annual "what we got right and wrong" report is a genuinely anticipated public event, not just an in-app feature.
- **Referral:** A meaningfully self-sustaining referral loop, still governed by the same no-cash-incentive, trust-triggered design established in Stage 3 — proof that the discipline held even once it stopped being necessary for survival.
- **Revenue:** A diversified, multi-tier model (consumer premium, family/household plans, B2B2C institutional/education partnerships) — but the core, free, evidence-and-honesty layer remains permanently free, as a stated, permanent commitment, not a current-stage constraint.
- **Education:** A recognized financial-literacy institution in its own right, potentially the largest single point of investing education for its core underserved demographics (teens, first-time investors, older adults new to apps).
- **Community:** A genuine, self-moderating, multi-tier community with real institutional memory of its own — long-tenured users mentoring new ones, echoing the platform's own Mentor stage (`MOBILE_PRODUCT_MASTERPLAN.md` §9) at a community level.
- **Content strategy:** A trusted public media presence in its category, competing on honesty and clarity rather than urgency or hot takes — the anti-CNBC, deliberately.
- **University strategy:** A standard, recognized part of campus financial-literacy programming at scale, potentially with a formal accreditation or certificate pathway.
- **Family strategy:** A default recommendation from parents to parents, and from teachers to families, for how a household introduces a young person to investing — the family use case has become a primary identity, not a feature.
- **International:** Meaningful presence across multiple markets, each genuinely localized (data coverage, language, and — critically — locally relevant evidence sources, not just translated US content).
- **KPIs:** Weekly Trust-Confirmed Users as the enduring North Star; long-run calibration accuracy; % of new users arriving via unpaid referral or reputation.
- **Leading indicators:** Multi-year cohort trust curves (does trust in year 3 exceed trust in year 1 for the same cohort — the ultimate test of the whole thesis).
- **Lagging indicators:** Brand trust surveys against named competitors; long-run retention and revenue durability through at least one full market cycle (a bear market is the only real test of whether "advisory, honest, and calm" survives contact with genuine user fear).

---

## A Note on What Never Changes Across Stages

Every stage above scales acquisition, revenue, and reach. Nothing above ever scales, dilutes, or renegotiates: the free evidence/confidence/uncertainty core, the no-dark-patterns rule, the honest track record (including the misses), or the advisory-only boundary. A growth playbook that has to compromise any of these to hit a later stage's numbers has, by definition, produced a different, worse company than the one this document is written for — and at that point, the correct response is to slow down, not to ship.
