# First 25 Users Profile
## Office of the Private Beta Readiness Board — ImpactOne

**Mandate:** Define the ideal first 25 users for the private beta — who should be invited, who should not, and why. This is the operational successor to `PRIVATE_BETA_PLAYBOOK.md` §1, restated here as the Board's own standard for what makes a cohort valid evidence rather than just enthusiastic testers.

---

## Who Should Be Invited

| Segment | Count | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| Complete beginners (16–25, at least 2 paired with a parent/mentor) | 5 | The hardest audience to satisfy honestly, and the one the product exists for |
| Long-term investors (28–40) | 5 | The core early-adopter profile most likely to use the product daily |
| Parents (35–55) evaluating it for a teenager | 5 | Tests the family use case directly and reads every explanation more carefully than most |
| Retired or near-retired investors (60+), new to finance apps | 5 | Stress-tests accessibility, pacing, and comprehension without prior app fluency |
| Experienced, deliberately skeptical self-directed investors | 5 | Fastest to notice templated content, inconsistent scoring, or an unearned claim — the cheapest possible way to catch exactly the failure modes this board cares most about |

---

## Who Should Not Be Invited, and Why

- **Anyone unwilling to commit roughly 10 minutes a day for the beta's duration** — a participant who can't engage regularly produces data too sparse to be useful evidence either way.
- **Press, public influencers, or anyone with a large public platform** — reputational risk during an admittedly unfinished private beta outweighs any early attention, and this board has direct, recent evidence that Critical findings can still surface even after real fixes have landed.
- **Anyone professionally employed by a direct competitor in a conflicted role.**
- **A minor without a paired, consenting adult account** — no appropriate account structure exists for an unaccompanied minor.
- **Anyone screened as unlikely to report a negative finding honestly** — a beta built on polite silence produces no usable evidence, which defeats its entire purpose.
- **Anyone personally close to the engineering team in a way likely to bias feedback toward encouragement over honesty.**

---

## Why This Composition, Specifically

Twenty-five people, deliberately spread across five segments of five, is large enough to surface real patterns and small enough that every single participant's specific experience can still be reviewed individually, every day, by name — the moment this cohort grows large enough that any single participant's experience gets lost in an aggregate, the Board has lost its ability to do the one thing a private beta is actually for: catching a real, specific problem before it reaches a stranger.
