# Product Metrics System
## Office of the Chief Product Strategist — ImpactOne

**Mandate:** Define the metrics that determine whether this company is actually succeeding — separated into four categories that must never be blended into one dashboard number, because each measures a fundamentally different question and a company that only tracks one of them can look healthy while quietly failing at the others.

---

## Product Metrics — Is the Product Working?

| Metric | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Time-to-first-insight | Does a new user reach real value within the 90-second design target (`MORNING_EXPERIENCE_BLUEPRINT.md`)? |
| Onboarding completion rate | Does the first-run experience actually complete, or do users drop before reaching value? |
| Daily/weekly opens | Is the habit loop actually forming? |
| Session length | Held to a *shorter-is-often-better* standard here — a growing session length can indicate confusion, not engagement. |
| Screens reached per session | Is the product being explored, or narrowly used out of habit alone? |
| Taps-to-action | Are core daily actions still within the 2-tap ceiling (`DESIGN_SYSTEM_V2.md` §21)? |
| Navigation efficiency | Percentage of sessions that reach the intended destination without a wrong turn or a dead end. |

---

## Trust Metrics — Does the Product Deserve to Be Believed?

| Metric | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Trust Score (four components) | The full model from `TRUST_SCORE_MODEL.md` — Stated, Behavioral, Resilience, and Advocacy Trust, tracked separately, never blended. |
| Calibration error | Of all claims stated at a given confidence level, what share actually turned out correct — the platform's single most important self-check. |
| Say-vs-do gap | The divergence between stated trust and behavioral trust for the same cohort in the same period — the earliest warning signal available. |
| Resilience delta | Trust before versus after a disclosed mistake — the clearest test of whether `TRUST_RECOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md` is being honored in practice, not just in policy. |
| Source verifiability rate | Percentage of claims shown to users that carry a real, checkable source — currently the single most important metric to move given known findings on this exact dimension. |

---

## Learning Metrics — Is the Platform Genuinely Getting Smarter?

| Metric | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Graded outcome count | How many recommendations have reached a real, graded result — the raw material every other learning metric depends on. |
| Calibration curve trend | Is the gap between stated confidence and real accuracy shrinking over time, cohort over cohort? |
| Recalibration proposals reviewed / applied | Is the platform actually updating itself from evidence, through the reviewed, attributed process `EVIDENCE_QUALITY_MODEL.md` §5 requires — or is evidence accumulating without ever changing anything? |
| World Memory lessons recorded | Is durable, multi-year institutional memory actually accumulating, or is every situation still being reasoned about from a blank slate? |
| Evidence quality distribution | Is the platform's evidence mix shifting toward higher-tier sources over time, or drifting toward cheaper, lower-quality ones as it scales? |

---

## Business Metrics — Is This a Durable Company?

| Metric | What it answers |
|---|---|
| Day-30 / day-90 / day-365 retention | The most fundamental test of whether the product delivers durable value, not just a good first impression. |
| Referral rate (trust-triggered, uncompensated) | Is trust actually compounding into advocacy, per `GROWTH_PLAYBOOK.md`'s stated model? |
| Cohort growth by acquisition source | Is growth arriving from durable, aligned channels (referral, education, community) or from channels that don't match the product's actual audience? |
| Premium conversion and premium-tier churn | Once monetization exists: is willingness-to-pay tracking with genuine trust (measured against the Trust Score), or with something else entirely — a premium cohort that churns faster than free users is an early warning, not just a revenue footnote. |
| Cost of acquisition vs. lifetime value | Standard business durability check, applied only once monetization is real enough to make it meaningful. |

---

## The One Rule That Governs All Four Categories

**No category is ever allowed to compensate for a failure in another.** Strong Business metrics alongside weak Trust metrics is not a healthy company — it is a company monetizing users faster than it is earning their trust, which `THE_100_YEAR_COMPANY.md` names as exactly the failure mode a durable company must never permit. Every quarterly review reads all four categories side by side, never a single blended "company health" score, because the whole point of separating them is to make it impossible to hide a weakness in one behind strength in another.
